## Trademark boundary

The [USPTO trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/) and [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/index) remain the authoritative starting points for a legal search. The launch search found similar spellings in unrelated categories, but did not establish legal availability in every country or software class.

Before commercial trademark registration, paid promotion, or a legal availability claim, obtain a jurisdiction-specific professional clearance search.
